

Power BI Sales Dashboard 📊
About the Project
This project is a Power BI dashboard that I created to explore sales data and understand business performance through interactive visualizations. It helped me practice presenting data in a simple way and identify useful insights about sales, products, customers, and business performance.
Dashboard Pages
1. Executive Overview
Provides a quick overview of overall business performance and important sales metrics.
Helps understand the key numbers and get a quick picture of the business.
2. Sales Trend Analysis
Explores sales trends and how sales performance changes over time.
Helps identify growth patterns and understand how sales are performing.
3. Product Performance
Focuses on product sales and compares the performance of different products.
Helps understand which products contribute more to overall sales.
4. Customer Analysis
Explores customer-related information and purchasing patterns.
Helps understand customer behavior and how customers contribute to business sales.
5. Salesperson Performance
Shows and compares the performance of salespeople.
Helps understand individual sales contributions and identify differences in performance.
6. Geographic Analysis
Explores sales performance across different geographical locations.
Helps understand which regions or locations contribute more to sales.
7. Budget vs Actual
Compares planned sales targets or budgets with actual performance.
Helps identify differences between expected and achieved results.
8. Customer Details
Provides a detailed view of customer-related information.
Helps explore individual customer records and understand the available customer data.
Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI

- Data Analysis
- Interactive Reports
